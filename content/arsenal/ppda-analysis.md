## What is PPDA?

Passes Allowed Per Defensive Action (PPDA) is a core metric for pressing intensity. A lower PPDA means a more aggressive press. Arsenal's 7.8 compares to a Premier League average near 11.2.

## The Mechanism Behind the Number

PPDA is produced by coordinated triggers across the front unit and midfield. Arsenal's counter-press behavior after turnovers is central to this profile.

## Where the System Gets Stretched

Arsenal's press is vulnerable against direct play, elite line-breaking passers, and late-game fatigue windows.
