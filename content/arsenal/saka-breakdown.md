## The Evolution of a World-Class Player

It's easy to forget that Bukayo Saka is still only 23. In 2024/25, Saka has posted **14 goals and 11 assists** from 32 appearances - but the advanced metrics reveal a more complete attacking profile.

## Shot Profile: The Clinical Upgrade

Saka's xG per shot has risen from 0.11 in 2022/23 to 0.15 this season. He's taking fewer speculative attempts and arriving in higher-quality positions.

Of his 14 goals, **7 came from inside the six-yard box** - a strong signal of improved movement and finishing context.

## The Dribbling Map

Saka's 58 completed dribbles from 72 attempts gives him an 80.6% success rate. More importantly, the location of his dribbles has shifted toward the half-space and penalty area.
